Jazz Auto - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Jazz Auto is a personal Android app operated by Paska ("I", "me"). It creates instrumental jazz music videos and uploads them to the account of the person using the app.

Information the app handles

  • TikTok and YouTube account authorization: when you log in, the app receives an access token and a refresh token. They are stored only on your device and are used only to upload videos you create.
  • API keys you enter (for example ElevenLabs and Pexels keys): stored only on your device.
  • Audio and video files: created and processed on your device. Files are uploaded only to the platform you choose (TikTok or YouTube).

What I do not do

I do not run a server for this app. I do not collect, receive, sell, share or analyze your personal data. The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

Third-party services

To work, the app sends requests directly from your device to: TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API), YouTube (Data API), ElevenLabs (music generation) and Pexels (stock footage). Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

I may update this policy; the effective date above will change when I do.
